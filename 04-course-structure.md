# Course Structure – Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This document defines the structural outline of the textbook.
It does not contain full content.

---

## Introduction
- Course overview and learning goals
- What is Physical AI and embodied intelligence

---

## Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)

### Chapter 1.1: Introduction to ROS 2
Intent: Introduce ROS 2 as middleware for robotic systems.

### Chapter 1.2: Nodes, Topics, and Services
Intent: Explain core ROS 2 communication primitives.

### Chapter 1.3: Python Agents with rclpy
Intent: Show how Python-based agents interact with ROS controllers.

### Chapter 1.4: URDF for Humanoid Robots
Intent: Explain robot description and kinematic structure.

---

## Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

### Chapter 2.1: Simulation Concepts
Intent: Explain physics simulation and digital twins.

### Chapter 2.2: Gazebo Environment Setup
Intent: Introduce robot simulation with physics and sensors.

### Chapter 2.3: Sensor Simulation
Intent: Simulate LiDAR, cameras, and IMUs.

### Chapter 2.4: Unity for Visualization
Intent: Introduce high-fidelity visualization and interaction.

---

## Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)

### Chapter 3.1: Isaac Sim Overview
Intent: Introduce photorealistic simulation and synthetic data.

### Chapter 3.2: Isaac ROS and Perception
Intent: Explain hardware-accelerated perception pipelines.

### Chapter 3.3: Navigation and VSLAM
Intent: Explain robot localization and path planning.

---

## Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)

### Chapter 4.1: Voice-to-Action Pipelines
Intent: Explain speech-to-command systems.

### Chapter 4.2: Cognitive Planning with LLMs
Intent: Translate natural language into robot actions.

---

## Capstone Project: Autonomous Humanoid

### Chapter C1: System Architecture
Intent: Define the end-to-end humanoid system.

### Chapter C2: Integrated Demo
Intent: Demonstrate perception, planning, and action.
